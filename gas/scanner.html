Camera QR scanner

Camera access is requested by this standalone HTTPS page, not inside the Apps Script frame. Allow camera access for project-bharat.github.io when Chrome asks; permission granted to script.google.com does not grant permission to this scanner.

Ready. Tap “Allow camera & start scanning”.

After a QR code is read, its value is sent to the GalleryPass Apps Script endpoint and returned to the original tab for appointment lookup. The Apps Script response is sent in no-cors mode, so this page cannot verify the server's response.
